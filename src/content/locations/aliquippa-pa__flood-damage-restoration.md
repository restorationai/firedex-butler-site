## Why Aliquippa Properties See Flood Damage Issues

Aliquippa sits along the Ohio River on terrain that slopes sharply from the hillsides down toward the water, and that geography matters when storms hit. Much of the borough's housing stock went up during the early and mid-20th century steel boom, which means a large share of basements were built with stone or block foundations never designed for modern stormwater volume. Combine that older foundation work with a municipal storm sewer system that predates current rainfall patterns, and you get a town where heavy rain events push water through foundation cracks and window wells long before the river itself crests. Flooding here tends to arrive in two waves: fast surface water from street runoff, followed hours later by groundwater seeping steadily through masonry that's been soaking it up the whole time.

## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Aliquippa

We start with extraction, pulling standing water out of basements and crawlspaces before it has a chance to wick further into framing and subfloor. On Aliquippa's older block-foundation homes, we pay close attention to the mortar joints and parging, since those surfaces hold moisture longer than poured concrete and can keep reading wet on a meter days after the visible water is gone. Once extraction is done, we set air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the room's actual moisture load rather than a generic equipment count, and we track drying progress with daily moisture readings instead of guessing at a timeline. For homes with finished basements, that often means pulling baseboard and a few inches of drywall to let the wall cavity dry properly, since trapping moisture behind an intact wall is how mold problems start two weeks after everyone thought the flood was handled.

## Aliquippa Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most flood claims in this area come down to the same question: was this rising water (often excluded or requiring separate flood insurance) or was it a sewer backup or interior pipe failure (typically covered under a standard homeowner's policy)? We document the source of the water, the affected materials, and the extraction and drying process with photos and moisture logs, because that paper trail is what determines whether a claim gets approved or kicked back for more information. ## Local note

One thing we watch for specifically in Aliquippa's older housing stock: homes built on the hillside sections often have a secondary drain or sump pit added decades after original construction, sometimes tied into a pump that was never upgraded along with the rest of the plumbing.
