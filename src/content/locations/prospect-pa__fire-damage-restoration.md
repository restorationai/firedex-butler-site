## Why Prospect Properties See Fire Damage Issues

Once a fire starts, tight lot lines and close-set structures mean smoke and heat can migrate into an attached garage or a neighboring wall faster than in a more spread-out suburb. ## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Prospect

We start with a walk-through to separate what's structurally sound from what isn't, because older framing can look fine and still have char weakening it from the inside.
