## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process

We start by identifying the source, whether that's a sump pump failure, a foundation crack letting in groundwater after a storm, or a supply line break in a mechanical room. Standing water gets pumped and extracted first, then we move to any finished materials, drywall, insulation, and flooring that have absorbed water and can't be salvaged. Drying follows the IICRC S500 water damage guidelines as a baseline, with moisture mapping on foundation walls so we know whether we're dealing with surface dampness or water that's worked into stone or mortar joints. On properties with well systems, we also check whether floodwater has reached a well pit or pressure tank before calling the space clear, since that's a detail easy to miss if you're not used to private water systems.
